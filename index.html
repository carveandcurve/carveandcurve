07 — Frequently Asked

Acoustic panels are wall or ceiling-mounted sound absorbers that reduce echo, flutter echo, and bass buildup — dramatically improving clarity for studios, podcasts, and home cinemas.

Every panel is handcrafted in our workshop in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. We install across Chennai, Chengalpattu, Kanchipuram and Tiruvallur, with shipping available further out.

Core is frameless and minimal; Artisan adds a solid Rubberwood frame; Signature adds bespoke dual-tone fabric and greater depth. See the full comparison.

Standard sizes range 2ft×4ft to 4ft×6ft, with fully bespoke dimensions from 1ft×1ft available on request.
