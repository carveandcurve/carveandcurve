Recording Studio Acoustic Treatment

Critical listening demands a room that doesn't lie to you.

Untreated studios suffer from three predictable problems: slap-back echo between parallel walls, bass mud accumulating in corners, and flutter echo smearing transient detail. Every one of these colours your mix — and colours the decisions you make while mixing, in ways that only become obvious on a different system.

Our studio treatment plans typically combine wall panels for lateral reflection control, corner bass traps to anchor the low end, and a cloud system above the mix position for vocal and imaging clarity.
