Acoustic panels are wall or ceiling-mounted sound absorbers that reduce echo, flutter echo, and bass buildup in a room. If you record, mix, take calls, or watch films in a space and it sounds harsh, boomy, or "echoey," acoustic treatment is what fixes that.

Every panel is handcrafted in our workshop in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, using Safe n Silent Pro Rockwool cores and solid Rubberwood framing. We install across Chennai, Chengalpattu, Kanchipuram and Tiruvallur, with shipping available further out. Read our story →
