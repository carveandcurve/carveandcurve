Home Theatre Acoustic Treatment

Cinema-grade sound means dialogue you understand and bass you feel — not bass that turns to mud.

A home theatre has a different job than a studio: it needs to handle wide dynamic range, keep dialogue intelligible at low volumes, and control bass energy in a typically smaller, more furnished room than a purpose-built cinema.

We treat the corners first, since low-frequency buildup is the most common complaint in home cinemas, then address ceiling reflections above the seating position for cleaner surround imaging.
